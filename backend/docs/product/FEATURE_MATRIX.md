# Purpose

This document defines which capabilities are available in each Loqi plan.

It serves as the single source of truth for Product, Engineering, Design, Sales, and Marketing.

Pricing, quotas, and numerical limits are intentionally excluded from this document. Those are defined separately in the Pricing Specification.

# Notes

## Feature Availability

This document defines **feature availability only**.

It intentionally does **not** define:

- AI generation limits
- Discovery credits
- Campaign limits
- Storage limits
- Rate limits
- Pricing

Those are specified in the Pricing Specification.

---

## Discovery Credits

Discovery Credits are only consumed for operations that incur an external cost to Loqi, including:

- Lead Discovery
- Company Discovery
- Contact Enrichment
- Company Enrichment
- Email Verification
- Phone Discovery

Searching a user's Workspace Knowledge Base never consumes Discovery Credits.

---

## Workspace Knowledge

The Workspace Knowledge Base is exclusive to paid plans.

Once imported, all searches against Workspace Knowledge are unlimited and never consume credits.

---

## Design Principles

This Feature Matrix follows the Loqi Product Constitution.

- Plans unlock **capabilities**, not arbitrary restrictions.
- AI is included in paid plans.
- Workspace data is never metered.
- External providers remain invisible to users.
- Every capability belongs to a product engine.
- Numerical quotas remain separate from capability definitions.

---

# Revision Policy

This document should only change when:

- A new product capability is introduced.
- A capability moves between plans.
- A new engine is added to the platform.

Pricing changes and usage limits **must never** require modifications to this document.
