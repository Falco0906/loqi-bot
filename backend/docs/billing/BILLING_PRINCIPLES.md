# Billing Principles

These principles define how Loqi monetizes.

They should remain stable even if pricing changes.

---

## Principle 1

Subscriptions unlock capabilities.

Users pay to unlock engines.

---

## Principle 2

Discovery Credits represent external costs.

Credits exist only for operations that cost Loqi money.

Examples:

- Lead discovery
- Email verification
- Company enrichment
- Phone lookup

---

## Principle 3

Workspace data is never metered.

Searching your own CRM should never cost credits.

Searching your own CSV should never cost credits.

Searching your own knowledge base should never cost credits.

---

## Principle 4

AI is included.

Paid plans should not make users think about tokens.

Users buy outcomes.

Not API usage.

---

## Principle 5

Storage creates lock-in.

Workspace Knowledge Base should feel generous.

Artificial storage limits should be avoided whenever economically reasonable.

---

## Principle 6

Provider choice is invisible.

Users interact with Loqi.

Never Apollo.

Never Hunter.

Never PDL.

Providers are implementation details.

---

## Principle 7

Credits are predictable.

Users should always know:

- why credits were consumed
- how many remain
- what action consumed them

There should never be hidden usage.
