# Loqi Upgrade Journey

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Architecture Frozen  
**Owner:** Product Team  
**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the philosophy behind customer upgrades within Loqi.

Upgrades should represent the natural evolution of a customer's business—not artificial product restrictions.

Every transition should solve a new problem that the customer has genuinely grown into.

This document exists to ensure that future pricing decisions remain aligned with customer value rather than revenue optimization alone.

---

# Core Philosophy

Customers should never feel forced to upgrade.

They should upgrade because their business has evolved.

Every pricing plan should provide a complete experience for its intended audience.

An upgrade should introduce new capabilities—not simply remove previous limitations.

---

# Customer Growth Journey

```
Evaluate

↓

Organize

↓

Discover

↓

Collaborate

↓

Scale
```

As businesses mature, their operational challenges change.

Loqi grows alongside those changing needs.

---

# Stage 1 — Evaluation

## Customer Mindset

"I've heard about Loqi. I want to see how it works."

---

## Business Situation

The customer has little or no investment in Loqi.

They are exploring whether the platform fits their workflow.

Their priority is understanding the product—not running production workloads.

---

## Product Goal

Allow customers to experience Loqi's core workflow with minimal friction.

The focus is education and confidence.

---

## Success Outcome

The customer understands:

- how Loqi works
- how AI improves outreach
- how campaigns are created
- how the platform fits into their workflow

Once they believe Loqi provides value, they naturally progress.

---

# Upgrade Trigger

"I want to use Loqi with my own sales data."

↓

Move to AI Workspace.

---

# Stage 2 — Organize Existing Leads

## Customer Mindset

"I already have leads. I need a better way to manage and engage them."

---

## Business Situation

The customer owns valuable prospect data.

Examples include:

- CRM records
- CSV files
- spreadsheets
- previous customers
- imported contact lists

The challenge is no longer finding people.

The challenge is using existing data effectively.

---

## Product Goal

Turn customer-owned data into an intelligent outbound workspace.

Customers organize knowledge, build campaigns and automate outreach using the information they already possess.

---

## Success Outcome

Loqi becomes the customer's primary sales workspace.

Their existing data is centralized, searchable and actionable.

---

# Upgrade Trigger

"My existing database isn't enough anymore."

↓

Move to Discovery.

---

# Stage 3 — Discover New Prospects

## Customer Mindset

"I need Loqi to continuously find new opportunities."

---

## Business Situation

The customer has exhausted their existing prospect database or wants to expand into new markets.

Finding qualified prospects has become their primary challenge.

---

## Product Goal

Provide one intelligent Discovery Engine that abstracts multiple external providers into a single experience.

Customers should focus on finding opportunities—not managing provider subscriptions.

---

## Success Outcome

Loqi becomes the primary source of new outbound opportunities.

The customer no longer relies on multiple discovery tools.

---

# Upgrade Trigger

"My sales process now involves multiple people."

↓

Move to Teams.

---

# Stage 4 — Collaborate

## Customer Mindset

"Our company uses Loqi together."

---

## Business Situation

Outbound sales is no longer performed by one individual.

Multiple users need access to:

- campaigns
- knowledge
- analytics
- workspaces
- reporting

Consistency becomes more important than individual productivity.

---

## Product Goal

Enable coordinated sales execution across an organization.

Focus areas include:

- collaboration
- governance
- permissions
- administration
- reporting

---

## Success Outcome

Loqi becomes the organization's shared sales platform.

Knowledge, campaigns and analytics are centralized across the team.

---

# Long-Term Growth

As organizations continue to grow, Loqi should expand with them.

Future capabilities may include:

- Enterprise administration
- Workflow automation
- Marketplace integrations
- Advanced AI agents
- Organization-wide analytics
- Compliance features

These additions should extend the customer's journey without changing its underlying philosophy.

---

# Upgrade Principles

Every upgrade should satisfy the following conditions.

## Solve a New Problem

Upgrades exist to address problems that did not exist at the previous stage.

---

## Preserve Existing Work

Customers should never lose progress when upgrading.

Campaigns, knowledge, analytics and history should continue seamlessly.

---

## Avoid Artificial Friction

Users should never feel pressured into upgrading because their existing plan became unusable.

Natural business growth—not frustration—should drive upgrades.

---

## Deliver Immediate Value

Customers should experience meaningful new capabilities immediately after upgrading.

The benefit should be obvious without additional explanation.

---

# Downgrade Philosophy

Customers should retain ownership of all Workspace data regardless of plan changes.

When capabilities become unavailable due to a downgrade:

- Existing data should remain intact.
- Historical campaigns should remain accessible.
- Workspace Knowledge should be preserved.
- Analytics should remain viewable where practical.

Where possible, capabilities should become read-only rather than destructive.

Downgrading should reduce functionality—not erase customer work.

---

# Non-Goals

The upgrade journey is not intended to:

- Maximize forced conversions.
- Create artificial feature gaps.
- Encourage unnecessary upgrades.
- Penalize customers for remaining on lower plans.
- Restrict customer-owned data.

The objective is sustainable customer growth—not pricing pressure.

---

# Guiding Principle

Customers should never think:

> "I have to upgrade."

They should think:

> "My business has grown, and Loqi has a plan designed for exactly where I am now."

Every upgrade should feel like the next logical step in a customer's journey, reinforcing Loqi as a platform that grows alongside the businesses it serves.
