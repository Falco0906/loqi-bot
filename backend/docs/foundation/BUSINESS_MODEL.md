# Loqi Business Model

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Architecture Frozen  
**Owner:** Founders / Product Team  
**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the business model of Loqi.

It explains:

- How Loqi creates value.
- How Loqi captures value.
- What customers pay for.
- What Loqi pays for.
- Which costs should be absorbed by the platform.
- Which costs should be passed to customers.

This document intentionally excludes pricing amounts.

Prices, quotas and plan limits are defined separately in the Pricing Specification.

---

# Business Philosophy

Loqi sells outcomes.

Not software.

Not AI.

Not API access.

Customers purchase Loqi because it helps them find, understand and reach the right people faster than existing workflows.

The customer should never think about:

- AI models
- Provider APIs
- Infrastructure
- Tokens
- Technical integrations

Loqi abstracts complexity into one intelligent workspace.

---

# Value Proposition

Loqi combines multiple disconnected sales workflows into one platform.

Instead of using separate tools for:

- Lead discovery
- CRM management
- AI writing
- Email outreach
- Campaign management
- Analytics

Customers use a single intelligent workspace.

The value of Loqi increases as more customer data, workflows and knowledge become centralized within the platform.

---

# Revenue Model

Loqi generates recurring revenue through subscriptions.

Certain platform capabilities also consume Discovery Credits due to external provider costs.

Revenue is generated from:

- Monthly subscriptions
- Annual subscriptions
- Discovery Credit purchases (future)
- Enterprise contracts
- Future platform extensions

Subscriptions remain the primary revenue source.

Credits exist to recover external operational costs—not to become the primary monetization strategy.

# Customer Segments

Loqi serves four primary customer groups.

## Evaluators

Users exploring Loqi.

Goal:

Understand the platform before purchasing.

Corresponding Plan:

Free

---

## Existing Data Owners

Organizations that already possess customer or prospect data.

Examples:

- Agencies
- Recruiters
- Sales teams
- Startups

Primary need:

Use existing data more intelligently.

Corresponding Plan:

AI Workspace

---

## Prospect Seekers

Organizations that require continuous lead discovery.

Primary need:

Find and enrich new prospects.

Corresponding Plan:

Discovery

---

## Collaborative Teams

Organizations operating outbound sales across multiple people.

Primary need:

Shared workspaces, governance and reporting.

Corresponding Plan:

Teams

---

# Value Capture Strategy

Different platform capabilities create value in different ways.

## Fixed Value

Capabilities whose cost does not increase significantly with usage.

Examples:

- Workspace
- AI workflows
- Templates
- Campaign builder
- Knowledge search
- Memory
- Dashboards

These capabilities are monetized through subscriptions.

---

## Variable Value

Capabilities whose cost increases with every request.

Examples:

- External lead discovery
- Company enrichment
- Email verification
- Phone lookup

These capabilities are monetized through Discovery Credits.

---

# Cost Structure

Loqi incurs two categories of costs.

## Fixed Costs

Infrastructure that supports the platform regardless of customer activity.

Examples:

- Servers
- Databases
- Authentication
- Monitoring
- Storage
- Development
- Customer Support

These costs scale gradually.

---

## Variable Costs

Costs directly associated with customer actions.

Examples:

- Lead provider APIs
- Enrichment providers
- Verification providers
- AI inference
- Third-party services

These costs increase proportionally with usage.

The platform should recover these costs through subscriptions or Discovery Credits.

---

# Billing Philosophy

Subscriptions unlock platform capabilities.

Credits cover external operational costs.

Customers should never feel like they are paying separately for every action.

The preferred customer experience is:

"I subscribed to Loqi."

Not:

"I purchased API requests."

---

# Discovery Credits

Discovery Credits exist solely to recover variable external costs.

Credits may be consumed for:

- Prospect discovery
- Company enrichment
- Contact enrichment
- Email verification
- Phone discovery

Credits are never consumed for:

- AI writing
- Campaign generation
- Workspace Knowledge search
- CRM searches
- CSV searches
- Internal workflows

Discovery Credits represent infrastructure costs—not premium features.

---

# Workspace Knowledge Strategy

Workspace Knowledge is a strategic asset.

Customer-owned knowledge should increase platform value without increasing customer cost.

Searching customer-owned information should always remain unlimited within plan boundaries.

The Workspace Knowledge Base should become increasingly valuable over time, encouraging long-term platform adoption.

---

# Provider Abstraction

Customers purchase Loqi.

Not Apollo.

Not Hunter.

Not People Data Labs.

External providers remain invisible implementation details.

Loqi is responsible for:

- Selecting providers
- Combining results
- Deduplicating data
- Ranking relevance
- Optimizing cost

Provider changes should never affect customer workflows.

---

# Customer Lock-In Philosophy

Loqi should earn retention through value.

Not through artificial barriers.

Retention should result from:

- Workspace Knowledge
- Historical campaigns
- AI memory
- Personalization history
- Analytics
- Team collaboration

Users should remain because leaving would reduce productivity—not because exporting data is difficult.

---

# Growth Strategy

Customer growth should naturally increase platform adoption.

Expected progression:

Evaluate

↓

Organize Existing Leads

↓

Discover New Leads

↓

Collaborate Across Teams

Each stage introduces new capabilities rather than restricting previous ones.

---

# Future Revenue Opportunities

Potential future revenue streams include:

- Additional Discovery Credit Packs
- Enterprise deployments
- Marketplace integrations
- Premium analytics
- AI agents
- Workflow automation
- Custom integrations

These opportunities should complement—not replace—the subscription model.

---

# Non-Goals

Loqi is not designed to:

- Sell customer data.
- Resell provider subscriptions.
- Charge customers for accessing their own information.
- Meter every AI interaction.
- Monetize unnecessary platform friction.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective is to make Loqi the operating system for outbound sales.

As organizations grow, Loqi should become the central platform where they:

- Store knowledge.
- Discover prospects.
- Build relationships.
- Automate outreach.
- Measure outcomes.
- Scale sales operations.

Revenue should grow because customers receive increasing business value—not because additional limitations are introduced.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every business decision should answer one question:

> Does this create significantly more customer value than the cost it introduces?

If the answer is no, the decision should be reconsidered.

Loqi should remain a value-first platform where monetization follows customer success—not the other way around.
