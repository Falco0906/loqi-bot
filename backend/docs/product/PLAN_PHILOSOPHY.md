# Loqi Plan Philosophy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Architecture Frozen  
**Owner:** Product Team  
**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the philosophy behind Loqi's pricing plans.

Pricing plans are not collections of arbitrary features.

Each plan exists to solve a specific customer problem at a particular stage of their sales journey.

This philosophy ensures that pricing remains consistent even as features evolve.

---

# Guiding Principles

## Plans Represent Customer Needs

Every plan should solve a complete problem.

Users should never feel like they are using an intentionally crippled version of the product.

Instead, every plan should feel complete for its intended audience.

---

## Upgrades Should Follow Business Growth

Customers should upgrade because their business requirements evolve.

Not because they hit frustrating limitations.

Every upgrade should unlock new capabilities that solve new problems.

---

## Capabilities Over Restrictions

Plans unlock engines and capabilities.

They do not exist to remove random features.

Artificial limitations should be avoided whenever possible.

---

## Clear Value Differentiation

Each plan must have one obvious reason to exist.

Customers should immediately understand which plan is meant for them.

If a customer cannot identify their plan within a few seconds, the pricing model has failed.

---

# Free

## Target Customer

People evaluating Loqi for the first time.

Examples include:

- Founders exploring outbound sales
- Freelancers
- Students
- Small businesses comparing tools
- Users curious about AI-powered outreach

---

## Customer Problem

"I've heard about Loqi, but I don't know if it's right for me."

---

## Product Goal

Allow users to experience Loqi's workflow without requiring payment.

The Free plan exists to answer one question:

> "Can Loqi improve the way I do outbound sales?"

It is **not** intended to run a business indefinitely.

---

## Success Metric

The customer experiences Loqi's core workflow and understands its value.

Examples:

- Creates a campaign
- Generates AI content
- Experiences lead discovery
- Connects a mailbox

The desired outcome is confidence—not long-term dependency.

---

## Why It Exists

Free reduces adoption friction.

It allows potential customers to evaluate Loqi before making a purchasing decision.

The Free plan should showcase Loqi's strengths while remaining economically sustainable for the platform.

---

# AI Workspace

## Target Customer

Organizations that already own prospect data.

Examples:

- Agencies
- Recruiters
- Startups with CRM databases
- Sales teams using spreadsheets
- Companies migrating from existing tools

---

## Customer Problem

"I already have leads. I need a smarter way to manage and engage them."

---

## Product Goal

Transform customer-owned data into an intelligent sales workspace.

AI Workspace focuses on:

- Organization
- Personalization
- Automation
- AI-assisted outreach

Loqi does **not** discover new leads in this plan.

Instead, it maximizes the value of the leads customers already own.

---

## Success Metric

Customers centralize their sales workflow inside Loqi.

Examples:

- Import existing contacts
- Build campaigns
- Generate personalized outreach
- Replace multiple disconnected tools

---

## Why It Exists

Many businesses already possess valuable prospect data.

Their biggest challenge is not finding leads.

It is using those leads effectively.

AI Workspace is designed specifically for this customer segment.

---

# Discovery

## Target Customer

Customers who want Loqi to actively discover new prospects.

Examples:

- Founders
- Sales representatives
- Business development teams
- Agencies acquiring clients
- Startups entering new markets

---

## Customer Problem

"I need Loqi to find qualified prospects for me."

---

## Product Goal

Provide a unified discovery experience without requiring customers to manage multiple external providers.

Discovery combines:

- Prospect discovery
- Enrichment
- Verification
- AI ranking

into one intelligent workflow.

Customers should never need to know which external provider supplied the information.

Loqi abstracts the entire discovery process.

---

## Success Metric

Customers consistently discover high-quality prospects while spending less time researching.

The platform becomes their primary source of outbound opportunities.

---

## Why It Exists

Lead discovery is fundamentally different from lead management.

Discovery introduces external costs through provider APIs and enrichment services.

Rather than exposing those complexities to customers, Loqi provides one unified discovery engine.

---

# Teams

## Target Customer

Organizations operating outbound sales collaboratively.

Examples:

- Sales teams
- Agencies
- Startups with multiple SDRs
- Enterprise organizations

---

## Customer Problem

"Our sales process involves multiple people."

---

## Product Goal

Provide collaboration, governance, visibility, and administration across the organization.

Teams focuses on:

- Shared workspaces
- Shared knowledge
- Shared analytics
- Shared campaigns
- Administrative control

---

## Success Metric

Organizations successfully manage outbound sales as a coordinated team rather than isolated individuals.

---

## Why It Exists

As organizations grow, sales becomes collaborative.

The challenge shifts from productivity to coordination.

Teams exists to solve organizational complexity rather than individual productivity.

---

# Upgrade Philosophy

Customers should progress naturally through Loqi as their business evolves.

```
Free
│
├── Evaluate Loqi
│
▼
AI Workspace
│
├── Organize Existing Leads
│
▼
Discovery
│
├── Acquire New Leads
│
▼
Teams
│
└── Scale Across The Organization
```

Every upgrade should correspond to a new business need.

No upgrade should exist solely because a previous plan became artificially restrictive.

---

# Non-Goals

Loqi's pricing model is **not** designed to:

- Encourage unnecessary upgrades
- Lock features behind arbitrary paywalls
- Monetize AI token usage
- Charge customers for accessing their own data
- Expose provider-specific pricing or complexity

---

# Long-Term Philosophy

Loqi should grow with its customers.

As businesses become more sophisticated, Loqi should unlock additional capabilities that support that growth.

Every plan should feel like a complete product.

Customers should upgrade because Loqi helps them achieve more—not because it prevents them from continuing what they were already doing.

This philosophy should remain consistent regardless of future pricing changes, feature additions, or platform evolution.
